> Accommodation and transport problems are increasing in many large cities. Businesses are encouraged to move to rural areas. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

These days, the problems with housing and transportation worsen in large urban areas. There is a push for firms to relocate to suburban and rural areas. From a personal perspective, albeit merits present, the advantageous influences are more prominent from the given phenomenon.

Undoubtedly, repositioning an establishment to a less populous region may pose various downsides. For one, the quantity of customers purchasing from a particular corporation may make or break it. A shift to a new venue can cause losses in potential or even loyal clients established over the years, which could be detrimental. Furthermore, by merely altering the location of a company, the entire logistical route could be disrupted. Moving out of the vicinity of related businesses could prove to be more costly in the long run, despite the current advantages they may have.

On the opposite end of the scale, however, the location-transfer plan of corporations might imply favourable conditions, both for the benefit of the establishment and society as a whole. With regards to the former, with a more advantageous location, as well as being able to expand quickly with the unused land, a business could flourish within a reasonable amount of time, offsetting the newly-found costs. As such, the extra money could be directly reinvested in the firm, resulting in a positive feedback loop. And as for the latter, having a successful enterprise in an area could be the key for it to thrive. China, for instance, often sets up cities in derelict lands for people and companies to live in and enhance them, thus redistributing the population density of the country. This would balance the accommodation and transformation problems of more occupied regions, and improve the morale of the citizens in general.

To summarise, while driving establishments to less populated areas is a positive tendency, the negative aspects are not to be neglected. However, from my perspective, the benefits far exceed the drawbacks.
