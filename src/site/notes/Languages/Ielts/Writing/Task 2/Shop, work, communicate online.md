> In the modern world, it is possible to shop, work and communicate online and live without any face-to-face contact with others. Is this a positive or negative development?


These days, individuals from many parts of the globe are able to go shopping, work, and contacts with other people through the internet. From a personal standpoint, albeit merits present, the advantageous influences are more prominent from the given phenomenon.

At one end of the spectrum, a host of repercussions can be witnessed from such an inclination. Chief among these is that humans are easily felt victim to numerous unlawful activities and wrongful behaviours all over the internet. As everyone can contact each other all around the world with certain levels of anonymity, crimes through social engineering are on the rise. The media frequently features stories about individuals or organisations falling prey to ransomware, phishing, or identity theft. For unwary users, these wrongdoings are hard to differentiate from legitimate behaviours.

At the other end of the spectrum, the beneficial impacts are more predominantly present. For one, living without face-to-face contacts has its benefits. Creating large social circles is not realistically possible in real life. With the help of networking services, however, it is certainly doable. With the growth of instant messaging, getting in touch and chatting is quicker than ever. Moreover, the internet provides an easy way to have multiple, separate personalities. No longer a person has to balance personal identity and work identity, as they can exist simultaneously. For another, keeping things online can lead to tremendous effects on the productivity of a society. People are free from the time spent on transiting to places, as they are able to order groceries, buy clothes, and work from home. This also has an added benefit of saving money in transportation costs, as well as decreased impacts on the environment from the reduced usage of vehicles.

To summarise, there are multiple advantages and disadvantages of doing things on the internet and living without face-to-face contact. Personally, I believe that the positive aspects outweigh the negative ones.
