> People in many countries spend more and more time far away from their families. Why does this happen and what effects will it have on them and their families?

These days, individuals in numerous nations are not spending enough time close to the members of their family. Personally, an array of reasons can be used to explain this phenomenon, and various measures against them will be clarified.

To commence, there are some explanations to why family time is decreasing. One of the biggest reason is that people are working or studying progressively more. While the costs of living are increasing, wages are not. As such, working overtime may be a necessity for some people, hence making them spend less time with their family. Students also have to study a lot more in order to get better-paying jobs. On top of that, because of globalisation, people are more likely to work or study in other nations than in the past. For example, an increasing number of pupils are considering to study overseas, especially in the US and the European union. That further drop time spent together with their families.

Multiple effects on their families and themselves are observed. For one, as family members are not as close-knitted as before, lots of issues may start to build up. A gap between the older and younger generations could begin to widen. And if families members cannot understand each other, the stability of such families are not secure anymore. But for another, there can be more opportunity for individuals. Spending time far away means they can learn more things about their works, studies, or hobbies, which may be more calming. Also, family time in these situations is also more appreciated, and not as often neglected as before. So each person can have more time with themselves while still building bonds with their family.

In conclusion, a multitude of contributors can be used to explain why people are opting to spend time elsewhere rather than with the members of their family. And while there are some negative effects, the positive effects are not to be disregarded.
