Given are tables delineating how various areas of the world, namely Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, and South & Central America, grew and made use of potatoes, in the year of 2006.

It is clear that the regions producing the highest quantities of potatoes were also those who consumed them the most. Also, the more populous areas also tended to use more of this produce.

Upon a closer investigation of the first table, Europe leaded the chart by a wide margin, with 96.1kg used by each individual. Following is North America, having its figure being only three fifth of that of the first place. The other regions had a lower rate of consumption, ranging between 25.8kg and 14.1kg per person.

Regarding the other table, Asia and Europe saw very similar statistics in terms of potatoes production, with 131.2 million tons for the former, and 126.3 million tons for the latter. North America came next, growing only about a quarter of hundred million ton. Africa and South and Central America once again found themselves at the bottom, witnessing figures below 17 million tons.
