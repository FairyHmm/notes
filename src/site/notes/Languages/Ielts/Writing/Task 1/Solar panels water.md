> Diagram of how water is produced by solar panels and water filters
 
![Solar panels water.png](/img/user/Languages/Ielts/Writing/Task%201/Media/Solar%20panels%20water.png)

Given is a pictorial description illustrating how drinking water is made using water purifiers solar panels.

In general, five key stages are involved in the manufacturing procedure, commencing with the preparation of solar panels to collect sunlight, and finalising with drinking water.

In the initial phase of the production process, solar panels with wattages ranging from 150W to 300W are utilised to catch light and convert it into electricity, prior to sending it through a solar controller. After which, energy is transmitted to an electric pump, which is placed inside a 10 to 50 meter deep well. Here, using direct current, the submerged pump is able to deliver the water into a filtering system. The procedure proceeds with the input material being purified by three separate stages of filter to sanitise water of any contamination, before it is sent to a storage tank. Ultimately, clean water can be extracted and ready to be used.
