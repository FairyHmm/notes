> The bar chart shows type of media to get daily news, by age group 2011

![Daily news media.png](/img/user/Languages/Ielts/Writing/Task%201/Media/Daily%20news%20media.png)

The bar chart compares how different age groups choose to update their everyday news in the year of 2011.

Overall, microblogging was the least utilised platform among the three surveyed, while radio was the most popular across several age brackets. Also, the use of social networking dropped proportionally with the age factor.

To commence, radio was the dominant media, accounting for close to 90% of the 18-29, 30-49, 50-64, and 65+ age groups. The only exception was for those aged 10 to 17. However, 40% of this group still used radio as their news deliver. Microblogging was always the least favored platform, with the usage varying between 25% and an unremarkable 10%.

Social networking saw 80% usage rates among people aged 10-17 and 18-29, making it one of the most popular medium for the younger demographic. However, it was less utilised by older individuals, representing 50% for the 30-49 age bracket, 40% for the 50-64 age group, and 25% for the oldest age group.
