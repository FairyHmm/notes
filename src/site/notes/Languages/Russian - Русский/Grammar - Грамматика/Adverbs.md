# Adverbs

- Adverbs add informaton to a verb, an adjective, or another adverb.
- Like English adverbs, Russian adverbs don't change their form.
    > Здесь <c>очень</c> приятная атмосфера. "The atmosphere is <c>very</c> nice here."
- If the sentence has several adverbs, then adverbs of time come first, while adverbs of place and manner follow.
    > <c>Утром</c> он <c>быстро</c> рассказал нам о своих планах. "In the <c>morning</c> he <c>quickly</c> told us about his plans."
