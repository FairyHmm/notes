## Cases

- Russian nouns appear in six cases: **nominative**, **accusative**, **genitive**, **dative**, **instrumental**, and **prepositional**.
- Russian nouns are divided into three groups depending on the case endings.

### Group 1: <c>-а -я</c> (except Nt <c>-мя</c>) 

| | Hard con <c>-а</c> | Soft con <c>-я</c> |
