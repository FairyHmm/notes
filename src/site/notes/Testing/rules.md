# Server Rules
### 1. Do not be rude, racist, offensive, derogatory, threatening, or incite violence in any way.
- This includes hate speech, promoting self-harm, and the use of racial slurs in any form.
- Do not target and harass others (such as annoying a specific person even after being told to stop).
- Do not maliciously instigate conflict and disorder.

### 2. Do not post or link sexual content, gore, or any NSFW/NSFL media.
- This includes the profile picture, banner, and status.
- Semi-NSFW content should be in spoilers.
- Do not let discussions get excessively vulgar in terms of language and the topics being discussed.

### 3. No cold advertising. This includes Discord servers, livestreams, channels, etc.
- The exception is in #self-promo.

### 4. Do not send unwanted DMs to others on the server.
- Sending DMs to advertise or to spread malicious content is also not allowed.

### 6. No excessive spamming, including characters, links, images, reactions, etc.
- Unnecessary text walls (that do not contribute to a discussion/conversation) can also be considered as spam.
- The exceptions are in #fun-spam and #spam-all-the-bread.

### 7. Do not ping others without good reason.
- Do not attempt to use @everyone or @here.

### 8. Do not send any messages containing viruses, phishing links, intellectual property infringements, or other malicious content.
- For intellectual property that isn't one's own, there may be a copyright infringement if it can't be easily accessed online for free.

### 9. Keep conversations on-topic for each channel. 
- Make sure to read the channel description and pins to know the topics of the channel.
- Offtopic discussion may be subject to deletion.
- Generally, avoid subjects like politics and religion.

### 10. Practice proper judgment and common sense, as poor behaviors not stated in the rules may be capable of justifying consequences.
- Being an unpleasant or unwanted member in general can lead to action taken, regardless of the number of actual rule violations.

▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬▬
 
- Failure to comply with these rules will result in a warning, timeout, kick, or ban (depending on the number of previous offenses, as well as the severity of the violation). Circumventing moderation actions will cause further consequences.

- [Discord’s Terms of Service](https://discord.com/terms) and [Community Guidelines](https://discord.com/guidelines) are also enforced. A known violation will result in an immediate ban.

- Encouraging rule violations or joining other members that are violating the rules is prohibited. If a rule violation is seen, please report it to the server administrators and moderators.
