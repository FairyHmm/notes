# Verbs

- One of the biggest differences between Russian and English grammar is probably the verb system.
- Russian has:
    - five tenses: the present, two past, and two future tenses
    - two moods: imperative and subjunctive
    - two aspects: perfective (PF) and imperfective (IPF)

## Conjugation
- There are two types of conjugation for Russian verbs:
    - The second conjugation includes all verbs with the infinitive ending <c>-ить</c> and several other verbs, for example: смотр<c>еть</c> "to see", держ<c>ать</c> "to hold", слыш<c>ать</c> "to hear", дыш<c>ать</c> "to breathe", ненавид<c>еть</c> "to hate".
    - Almost all other verbs belong to the first conjugation.

## To be

- As you already know, the verb "to be" is usually omitted in the Russian present tense. Thus, verbs of position are used to specify or describe the position of an object.

| | Already in position | Moving into position | Putting sth in position |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| to sit | сидеть | садиться<br>сесть | сажать<br>посадить |
| to stand | стоять | вставать<br>встать | ставить<br>поставить |
| to lie | лежать | ложиться<br>лечь | класть<br>положить |
| to hang | висеть | вешаться<br>повеситься | вешать<br>повесить |

> Где мои ключи? - Они лежат на столе. "Where are my keys? - They are (lying) on the table."

# Tenses
## Present

- In contrast to English, there is only one present tense, which can be translated both as I do and I'm doing.
- Cue words within the context (such as сейчас "now", вчера "yesterday", and каждый день "every day") will help you understand whether the action is ongoing or habitual.

### Usage

- an action happening at the time of speaking
- repeated action or a habit
- an action that started in the past and is still continuing

### Forming the Present
- Determine the conjugation type of the verb:
    > читать,  first conj "to read"
- Determine the stem of the verb:
   - Take the <c>Я</c> form of the verb and remove the last letter:
        > я чита<c>ю</c> → я чита "I read"
    - Take the <c>ТЫ</c> form of the verb and remove the three last letters:
        > ты чита<c>ешь</c> → ты чита "you read"
- Add an ending according to the conjugation type:
    > мы чита<c>ем</c> "we read"

## Past

- The past is the most common tense in Russian, and it is quite simple to form.

### Usage
- an action or an event at any point in the past

### Forming the past
- Determine the stem of the infinitive by removing <c>-ть -ти -чь</c>:
    > чита<c>ть</c> → чита- "to read"
- Combine the stem with the suffix <c>-л</c>:
    > я чита<c>л</c> "I read"
- Add the ending:

| Nt | <c>-ло</c> | Бы<c>ло</c> лето "It was summer" |
| Pl | <c>-ли</c> | Они гуля<c>ли</c> "They were walking" |
