> More and more plastic waste is polluting the world's city, countryside, and ocean. What problems will it cause? What measures should be taken to solve these?

These days, plastic has become a major concern for the environment, having been polluting both land and water. A multitude of contributors are to take the blame for the given phenomenon, and potential solutions will be clarified.

To commence, there are some explanations of how plastic waste is contaminating the ecosystem. One of the biggest reasons is that most types of plastic are not biodegradable, meaning they take a lot of time to breakdown, especially in water. Some of which take up to several centuries to completely deteriorate. As a result, they are frequently found in the ocean and soil, harming the organisms living in them, or causing difficulty building on them. For example, marine creatures are frequently seen being trapped inside plastic bags, restricting their movement. On top of this, the decomposition of plastic waste produces microplastics. These tiny plastic particles accumulate toxins, are hard to filter, and may go inside the bodies of animals. And this directly injures humans and other species.

Though plastic pollution is concerning, various actions can be taken. For one, discouraging the use of single-use plastic containers is a straightforward yet effective approach. The general public is one of the biggest offenders for releasing large amounts of plastic waste into the environment. Educating about this behavior, along with restraining the use of plastic, could help reduce this. California, for instance, charges a small fee for reusable bags, so people would reuse old ones. For another, promoting the use of alternatives is also a viable measure. Materials like wood and metal, while initially more expensive, are recyclable, and hence less likely to be thrown away, harming the ecosystem.

To summarize, the properties of plastic could majorly damage the environment, in addition to aggravate the health of mankind and animals. Interventions from higher authorities, together with supporting eco-friendly substitutes, can help alleviate the situation.
