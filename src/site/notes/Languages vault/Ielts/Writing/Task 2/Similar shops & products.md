> Many different countries have most shops and products that are the same. Some people consider it a positive development, while others think it is a negative development. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

In today's era of globalisation, branching out to others countries so that one's stores and goods can be found all around the world is a popular procedure. Some people support this trend, whereas others regard this as a negative practice. This essay will discuss the pros and cons of these viewpoints before giving opinion in the conclusion.

To begin with, it has become more convenient for customer to purchase products of various chains around the world. Specifically, merchandises of big corporations are often high in quantity and easy to find. As they have to compete with local brands, the pricing is kept to a minimal, while the quality and technology are generally more developed. Furthermore, consumers also appreciate the variety of purchasable. The availability of similar options promote competition, leading to products of higher quality. For these reasons, extending to other regions can be seen as beneficial to local citizens.

At the other end of the spectrum, it is often criticised that international brands tend to overshadow local or smaller ones. They may try to draw customer away from local shops, which can be seen as detrimental for local economy. For instance, Amazon - one of the biggest online stores, often divert consumers away from smaller retailer before absorbing them. Another reason is that there would be a lack of variety among commodities throughout the world.  If goods in a country is identical to those of another, it discourages travelling and purchasing, and in turn, hurt the economy.

In conclusion, while expanding products to other nations is a positive tendency, the negative aspects are not to be neglected. However, from my perspective, the benefits far exceed the drawbacks.
