> Houses are essential for people. Some people think that the government should offer free houses for people who cannot afford to pay for it. Do you agree or disagree with this statement?


It is not hard to see, that houses are vital parts in each individual's life. There exists an opinion, stating that people without the ability to afford a shelter deserve the housing offer free-of-charge by the national authorities. This essay shall present the personal view in favor of the stated notion.

Admittedly, the subsidisation of roofs for a portion of the population is not without various ramifications. For starters, providing all poor citizens with places of residence may not be a feasible task. As the government has to care for numerous things, some of which are healthcare, infrastructure, and education, only a selected portion of individuals are given shelters. And this directly leads to another point, namely divisions in society. Only providing help to some individuals may create a sense of inequality at the national level. This might cause public outrage and dissatisfaction among people unable to obtain aid.

On the opposite end of the scale, supporting those needing help with housing might be of greater significance, in terms of improving overall societal well-being, as well as stimulating the economic situation. With regards to the former, providing affordable housing options can alleviate the burden on families struggling to make ends meet. Finland, for instance, having been approaching to eliminate homelessness, is also considered one of the happiest countries in the world for several years. By ensuring access to safe and stable housing, it not only addresses immediate needs, but also contribute to long-term social stability. And as for the latter, investing in housing assistance programs can have a ripple effect on the economy. When individuals have secure housing, they are more likely to be productive members of society, able to focus on education, employment, and personal growth. This, in turn, boosts overall productivity and gives way to a positive cycle of economic development.

To summarise, while aiding citizens with shelters when they cannot is a positive tendency, the negative aspects are not to be neglected. However, from my perspective, the benefits far exceed the drawbacks.
