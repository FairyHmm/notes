> The diagram below shows how to recycle organic waste to produce fertilise.

![](../../../_resources/Picture1-1.png)

Given is a pictorial description illustrating how compost is recycled from biowaste.

In general, five key stages are involved in the recycling procedure, commencing with the preparation of a plastic container, and finalising with organic fertiliser.

In the initial phase of the recycling process, a container with roughly two cubic metres in size made out of plastic is utilised, with some holes poked in the side enabling the flow of air. After which, layers of organic input materials, such as paper, grass, or food, each measuring about 15 centimetres in height, are placed within the container. In the next step, hot water and nitrogen are added, supplying the pile with temperature and a catalyst for the decomposition, prior to sealing the cap of the container. Here, the waste mix slowly decays, with excess heat escaping from the holes in the side. Ultimately, after six months, the resulting fertiliser can be removed from the container, ready to be used in a garden.
