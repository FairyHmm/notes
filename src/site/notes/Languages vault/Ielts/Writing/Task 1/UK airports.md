> The table below shows the number of visits to selected countries from four UK airports in 2013.

![e3f9d6041c1e4861831c96b534a95f03.png](/img/user/resources/e3f9d6041c1e4861831c96b534a95f03.png)

Given is a table comparing how trips to four separate airports in the UK differed among visitors from six distinct nations in the year of 2013.

It is clear that there were considerable variations in the numbers of people going to the UK among airports. Also, Gatwick was the most popular airport, while Heathrow received the least number of travellers.

Looking more closely, Spain dominated the chart with the most trips to the UK at all but one airport, with almost 1.9 million flights to Gatwick airport, its biggest destination to the UK. Ireland and Canada followed at 2nd and 3rd, respectively. With regards to the former, the data ranged between 111 thousand (Manchester airport), and 278 thousand (Heathrow airport). And as for the latter, there were 187 thousand visits to Heathrow, 91 thousand for Gatwick, and 50 thousand for Manchester, while Stansted saw no data.

In comparison, the remaining countries observed less statistics. Austria had 148 thousand travellers coming to the UK via Gatwick, while the other airports experienced between 60 thousand and 100 thousand less trips from this country. Heathrow was the most popular airport to the UK for by the Finns (58 thousand visits), though Manchester was not far behind (53 thousand trips). And as for Lithuania, the figures were 33 thousand, 15 thousand, and 1 thousand to Stansted, Gatwick, and Heathrow, respectively, while no data was observed for Manchester airport.
