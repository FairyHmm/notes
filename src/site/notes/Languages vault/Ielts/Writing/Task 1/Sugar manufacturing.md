The process of manufacturing sugar begins with growing the sugar canes, which takes 12-18 months on average. In the second stage of the procedure, the fully grown sugar canes are harvested manually, using either a sickle or a harvester. The harvested sugar canes are put through a crusher to produce sugar cane juice in the next step. The fourth step of the sugar manufacturing process involves purifying the juice by with a limestone filter to remove any remaining impurities.

In the fifth stage of the process of producing sugar, the purified juice is put into through a high-temperature evaporator, causing water to evaporate and leaving syrup. The sugar manufacturing process continues with the syrup being centrifuged, separating the sugar crystals from the syrup due to their different densities. The seventh stage of the sugar production involves drying and cooling the formed sugar. Finally, the sugar is packed and distributed to stores.
