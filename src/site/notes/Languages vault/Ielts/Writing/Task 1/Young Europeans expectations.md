> The bar chart shows expectations to change of European young people in the next 20 years in 5 different areas 
> 
![098c930b717942e8855bf63e2f7f1909.jpg](/img/user/resources/098c930b717942e8855bf63e2f7f1909.jpg)

The bar compares young Europeans views towards changes over the following 20 years in five distinct aspects, namely health, air quality, water quality, food quality, and people communication.

It is clear that the majority of participants do not have high expectations for the improvement of most of the categories mentioned, aside from people communication. Of which, air quality is the most concerned about, having the lowest score.

To commence, people communication is predicted to improve in the next 20 years, with a support rate of about 65%. Opinions on food quality are divided almost evenly, with just under half believing it would improve, and the other half holding the opposite view.

By contrast, the remaining aspects all have below-average anticipations. Both health and water quality have similar predictions, with about 60% of young people forecasting deterioration. Meanwhile, air quality is the least promising aspect, with only 23% of those surveyed holding positive opinions.
